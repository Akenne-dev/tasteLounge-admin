⚙️ Store Settings

Delivery

Used by the cart, checkout and the free-delivery progress bar on each dish.

0 = standard delivery is free.
0 = hide the Express option at checkout.
0 = no free-delivery offer.
Shown on each dish page: "Hot at your door in …"

Opening hours

Customers can browse any time, but can only check out while you're open. Also drives the Open/Closed badge on the home page.

Hours follow the kitchen's clock, whatever time zone the customer is in. Nigeria = Africa/Lagos.
Closing after midnight? Set the closing time earlier than the opening time, e.g. 18:00 → 02:00.

Pause orders

For busy times. Staff can also flip this from the Dashboard.

Loyalty points

Customers earn points on food (not delivery). Points are added when you mark an order Delivered, and can be spent at checkout.

Payment methods

Which Paystack options customers see. Leave all unticked to allow everything switched on in your Paystack account.

Photo uploads

Lets you upload dish photos and website pictures instead of pasting links. Uses Cloudinary's free plan.

  1. Create a free account at cloudinary.com.
  2. On the Cloudinary dashboard, copy your Cloud name.
  3. Go to Settings → Upload → Upload presets → Add upload preset, set Signing mode to Unsigned, save, and copy the preset name.
  4. Paste both below and press Save.
Only admins can see these details.

Deals banner

The red sliding banner at the top of the Foods page. Leave it empty to hide the banner.
